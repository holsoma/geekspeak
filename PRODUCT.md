# GeekSpeak

## Register

product

## Users

People learning everyday tech jargon, including applied AI students. They want to follow conversations about AI, programming, databases and cloud computing without looking up every unfamiliar word.

## Product Purpose

Teach selected terms through short lessons, concrete examples and questions. Guests can start immediately. GitHub accounts save progress across devices and can opt into the leaderboard.

## Brand Personality

Curious, clear and approachable. The homepage presents conversation cards on a learning desk. Preserve the established speaking-head symbol, colours and typography documented in DESIGN.md.

## Anti-references

Avoid exhaustive acronym lists, streak pressure, timers, fake leaderboard entries, loading theatre, scroll hijacking and automatic sound. The design references are Table Tales and House of Giants; their artwork and code are not copied.

## Design Principles

- Turn unfamiliar tech language into explanations learners can use.
- Keep examples concrete and the writing direct.
- Let people learn at their own pace.
- Preserve learner progress and keep public participation optional.
- Keep the brand recognisable across the website and project assets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access, visible focus and reduced-motion support. Keep learning pages still at rest. Check desktop and 390 px mobile layouts for clipping and overflow.
